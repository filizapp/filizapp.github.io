Widgets & Shortcuts

Log habits and check on Filiz without opening the app. iOS lets you add these yourself in a few taps.

Home Screen widgets

See what's left today and check habits off with one tap.

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Filiz. Swipe to choose the small Filiz widget or the medium Today widget.
  4. Tap Add Widget, then Done.

💡 The medium widget has buttons: tap + to add a glass, or ✓ to mark a habit done.

Lock Screen widgets

Your progress and streak right on the Lock Screen.

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize and choose Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Add Widgets and choose Filiz.
  3. Tap the ring, the progress bar, or both.
  4. Tap Done.

Water in Control Center

Add a glass of water from anywhere, even inside another app.

  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
  2. Tap + in the top-left corner, then Add a Control.
  3. Find Filiz and tap Log Water.
  4. Drag the control's corner to make it wider, so it shows your glasses.

💡 Needs iOS 18 or later. The drop fills up as you drink.

Lock Screen buttons and the Action button

Swap the flashlight or camera button for one-tap water.

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  2. Tap − on the flashlight or camera button, then tap +.
  3. Choose Log Water from Filiz and tap Done.
  4. Action button (iPhone 15 Pro and later): Settings › Action Button › Controls › Log Water.

Live Activity and Dynamic Island

Filiz stays with you at the top of the screen and on the Lock Screen.

  1. It starts by itself when you leave the app and habits are left today (5 am to 10 pm).
  2. Touch and hold the Dynamic Island to open it, then tap +1 or ✓.
  3. When your day is done, Filiz pops up to celebrate.

💡 Turn it off any time in the app: Settings › Filiz in the Dynamic Island.

Apple Watch

Check habits off from your wrist and put Filiz on your watch face.

  1. Open the Watch app on your iPhone and scroll to Available Apps.
  2. Tap Install next to Filiz.
  3. To add a complication, touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, then pick Filiz.

💡 Reminders show up on your watch on their own when your iPhone is locked.

Siri and Shortcuts

Log a habit with your voice.

  1. Say “Hey Siri, log Drink Water in Filiz.”
  2. Or ask “How am I doing in Filiz?”
  3. Find more in the Shortcuts app under Filiz.

Done right from a reminder

No need to open the app when a reminder arrives.

  1. Touch and hold the reminder, then tap Mark done.
  2. Water can remind you up to four times a day, other habits twice. Set the times in the habit's edit screen.
  3. Once a habit is done, the rest of today's reminders for it stop.
